# Communication Guide
The skill creator is used by people across a wide range of technical familiarity — from experienced engineers to first-time terminal users.

## Defaults

- "Evaluation" and "benchmark" are borderline but OK
- "JSON" and "assertion" — only use without explanation if the user clearly knows what they are
- Briefly explain terms when in doubt

## Signals to watch for

- Casual phrasing, typos, "what's a terminal?" → non-technical user; use plain language and analogies
- References to IDEs, APIs, schema files → technical user; speak directly

## General principle

Pay attention to context cues. When in doubt, define terms with a short parenthetical and move on.
